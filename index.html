How a celebration cake comes together

From the Oven to the Counter

Cakes at Jan are finished by hand and put straight into the display case on Station Road. If you need one for a birthday or a family gathering, the way it works is simple:

  1. Drop by the counter — see what is in the case today.
  2. Say the occasion — birthdays and custom cakes are made to order.
  3. Order ahead — give the shop time to make yours, then collect it from Station Road.
